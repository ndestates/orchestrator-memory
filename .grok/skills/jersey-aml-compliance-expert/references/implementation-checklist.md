# Jersey AML/CFT/CPF Implementation Checklist (MLO + POCL)

## Governance
- [ ] Appoint MLRO and MLCO (or use approved AMLSP where permitted)
- [ ] Business-wide risk assessment completed and documented
- [ ] Written AML/CFT/CPF policies, procedures and controls
- [ ] Independent audit / testing function (proportionate to size/risk)
- [ ] Staff training programme (initial + ongoing, role-specific)

## Customer Lifecycle
- [ ] Customer / business risk assessment for every new relationship
- [ ] CDD performed before establishing relationship or carrying out occasional transaction
- [ ] EDD triggered and completed where risk factors present
- [ ] Source of funds / wealth obtained and verified where required
- [ ] Ongoing monitoring of transactions and the relationship
- [ ] Periodic reviews triggered by risk or time

## Record Keeping & Systems
- [ ] Records retained for required period (generally 5+ years)
- [ ] Audit trail for CDD, risk assessments, decisions, SARs, training
- [ ] Systems support transaction monitoring and alert escalation
- [ ] Secure, access-controlled storage for sensitive AML information

## Reporting
- [ ] Clear internal escalation process to MLRO
- [ ] External SARs filed promptly with JFCU when required
- [ ] Consent process followed where applicable
- [ ] No tipping off

## Registration & Supervision
- [ ] Scope analysis against current Schedule 2 of POCL performed
- [ ] Registration with JFSC completed (where required) before commencing activity
- [ ] Notifications and returns made to JFSC as required
- [ ] Respond to JFSC supervisory requests

## Data Protection Interface
- [ ] AML processing has lawful basis under DPJL (usually legal obligation)
- [ ] Data minimisation applied to CDD/SAR records
- [ ] Retention aligned with both regimes

Use together with the main skill, the latest JFSC AML/CFT/CPF Handbook, and legal advice tailored to your specific activities.
