# Jersey AML/CFT/CPF — Official Sources

## Primary Legislation
- Proceeds of Crime (Jersey) Law 1999 (POCL): https://www.jerseylaw.je/laws/current/l_8_1999
- Money Laundering (Jersey) Order 2008 (MLO): https://www.jerseylaw.je/laws/current/ro_20_2008

## Supervisor
- Jersey Financial Services Commission (JFSC)
  - AML/CFT/CPF legislation page: https://www.jerseyfsc.org/industry/financial-crime/amlcftcpf-legislation/
  - AML/CFT/CPF Handbook (Codes of Practice + Guidance) — always use the latest version

## Financial Intelligence Unit
- Joint Financial Crimes Unit (JFCU) — receives Suspicious Activity Reports (SARs)

## Key Related Laws
- Terrorism (Jersey) Law 2002
- Sanctions and Asset-Freezing (Jersey) Law 2019
- Non-Profit Organizations (Jersey) Law 2008 and related orders
- Proceeds of Crime (Supervisory Bodies) (Jersey) Law 2008

## Guidance
- JFSC AML/CFT/CPF Handbook sections (Introduction, CDD, EDD, Monitoring, Record Keeping, MLRO/MLCO, SARs, etc.)
- JFSC Notices under the MLO (e.g. Article 9A, Article 10 regarding AMLSPs)
- JFSC guidelines on interpretation of POCL Article 36 (failure to disclose)

## Best Practice
Perform a formal scope analysis against current Schedule 2 of POCL. Registration with the JFSC is mandatory for most in-scope businesses. Always refer to the current Handbook and law text. This skill is an aid only — obtain specific compliance advice for your business model.
